Tune – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Andre Piton

Summary

Tune is an app for teachers and tutors to manage students, lessons, payments and documents. Your data stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

The information you enter (students, contact details, lessons, notes, payments, packages, documents, email templates, branding and settings) is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. The developer cannot access it.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, Tune connects directly from your device to Google services: Google Calendar (to create and update lesson events and Google Meet links), Google Drive (to store backups and documents in your own Drive) and Gmail (to send emails you or your automations create, from your own account). Data travels only between your device and Google; it never passes through our servers. Sign-in tokens are managed by Google Sign-In on your device. Tune's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: data is used only to provide the features described above, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Notifications, Live Activities and widgets

Reminders, Live Activities and widgets are generated locally on your device. No data is sent to us.

Photos

If you pick an image (for example a logo), Tune accesses only the image you select. It is stored on your device.

Your students' data

You decide what to record about your students. You are responsible for having a legal basis to do so and for informing them, according to the laws that apply to you. Emails sent through Tune are sent from your Gmail account.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or share personal data. The only third party involved is Google, and only if you sign in. Google's own privacy policy applies to data stored in or sent through your Google account.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. To stop Tune's access to your Google account, sign out in the app or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files Tune saved to your Google Drive can be deleted from Drive at any time.

Children

Tune is intended for teachers and tutors and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
